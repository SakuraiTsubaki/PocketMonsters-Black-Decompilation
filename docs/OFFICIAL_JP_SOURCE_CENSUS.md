# Japanese Official Source Census — Generation V

## Purpose

This census enumerates surviving **Japanese first-party/official web material** for Pokémon Black, White, Black 2, and White 2. It is a source inventory, not a claim that the whole public-source survey is complete.

The Japanese releases are the comparison baselines for the regional survey. Every page below is preserved as evidence of what Nintendo / The Pokémon Company publicly documented at the time.

## Status vocabulary

- **Enumerated-core** — the known core HTML branch has been itemized from the surviving official index/site map.
- **Enumerating** — a live/archived branch exists but all children/assets/notices are not yet itemized.
- **Registered** — exact official page identified; deeper descendants may still exist.
- **Archived/partial** — only partial or archived evidence survives.

## E. Open Japanese-official branches — mandatory follow-up

The following are explicitly **not complete**:

1. Nintendo BW `topics/file/vol_xx/fileyy.html` full sequence.
2. B2W2 `news/` individual notice/campaign child pages and external destinations.
3. Pokémon.co.jp BW historical announcements not represented by the surviving core sitemap.
4. PGL / Dream World maintenance, renewal, campaign, competition and termination-related Japanese notices.
5. Japanese Mystery Gift, movie, store, tournament, magazine and campaign distribution announcements.
6. Official promotional videos, TV commercials, downloadable media and image assets with original metadata.
7. Official guidebook/soundtrack/product pages and publisher-authorized guide material.
8. Nintendo/Pokémon support notices concerning communication compatibility, maintenance and shutdown.
9. Internet Archive captures of official Japanese pages no longer live.
10. Physical Japanese box/cart/manual scans by revision where provenance is adequate.
